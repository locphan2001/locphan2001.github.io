With a background in mathematics and computer science, I am interested in developing computational methods and algorithms to address complex problems in operations research and related fields, from mathematical foundations to practical implementation.

My work focuses on mathematical models and algorithms, particularly for combinatorial optimization problems. I am also interested in exploring the connections between classical methods and emerging technologies such as Quantum, HPC, and AI, and how they can be integrated.

On the application side, I aim to turn these methods into efficient and practical solutions for real-world problems, while evaluating their performance and scalability across different applications and problem settings.

- *Computational Experiments*
